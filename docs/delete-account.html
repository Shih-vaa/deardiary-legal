Delete Your DearDiary Account

DearDiary · Account and data deletion

This page explains how to delete your DearDiary account and what happens to your data. Deletion is free and does not require reinstalling the app.

How to delete your account

You can delete your DearDiary account and profile directly from inside the app:

  1. Open the DearDiary app on your device.
  2. Go to the Profile section.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account and profile are deleted immediately.

Can't open the app? Email deardiarytesting@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and we will delete it manually within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account credential (email/password, or the link to your Google account).
  • Your profile information: display name, bio, age, and profile photo.

These are removed from our backend provider (Supabase) and are not recoverable once deleted.

What is not deleted by this action

DearDiary stores your diary content on your own device, and any Google Drive backup in your own Google Drive — not on our servers. Deleting your account does not remove these. To clear them yourself:

  • Local diary entries: uninstall the DearDiary app, or clear its data from your device's app settings.
  • Google Drive backup (if enabled): remove the DearDiary app data from your Google Drive, or revoke DearDiary's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Retention

Account and profile data are deleted immediately when you delete your account in the app. If you request deletion by email instead, we process it within 7 days. Encrypted database backups held by our provider are purged within 30 days.
