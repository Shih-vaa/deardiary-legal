Delete Your DearDiary Account

Request permanent deletion of your DearDiary account and the data associated with it — no app install required.

What gets deleted

  • Your account credential (email/password or Google sign-in link).
  • Your profile — name, bio, age, and photo, stored with our backend provider, Supabase.

This does not include diary entries stored locally on a specific device, or a Google Drive backup already sitting in your own Drive — see the note below.

How to request deletion

Email us from the address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete My Account". Include your account email if it differs from the sending address.

Email apoorvactor@gmail.com

We process deletion requests within 30 days and will confirm by email once it's done.

What this doesn't cover

Diary entries live only on your own device's local storage — we never receive them, so there's nothing on our servers to delete on that front. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data from your device's app settings, removes them from that device.

If you enabled Google Drive backup, that backup lives in your own Google Drive (Google's app-private storage area), not on our servers. Remove it directly from Google Drive, or by revoking DearDiary's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

You can also delete your account directly from the app: open Profile and tap Delete Account at the bottom — no waiting required.
