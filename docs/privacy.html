Privacy Policy — DearDiary

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy was drafted by auditing the app's actual code paths — what data it touches, where it sends it, and what third-party SDKs are wired in — not written from a template.

Summary

DearDiary is a journaling app. Your diary entries, doodles, and images are stored only on your device unless you explicitly turn on Google Drive backup. Creating an account (email or Google Sign-In) stores your login and basic profile info (name, and whatever else you choose to add) with our backend provider, Supabase. Crash reports go to Sentry. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

Information We Collect

Account information. If you sign up with email, we store that email and an encrypted password (handled by Supabase Auth). If you use "Sign in with Google," we receive your Google account's basic identity (email, name) via OAuth, along with a token scoped for Google Drive's app-private storage.

Profile information. Anything you enter on the Profile screen — display name, bio, age, profile photo — is stored in our database (Supabase). All of these except your name are optional.

Diary content. Everything you write, draw, or paste into a diary — text, doodles, embedded images, page titles — is stored locally on your device in a private, sandboxed database. We do not receive or have access to this content unless you turn on backup.

Backup (optional, off by default). If you enable Google Drive backup, your entire diary is uploaded to a hidden, app-private folder in your own Google Drive (Google's appDataFolder), which only this app, authenticated as you, can read. It does not appear in your regular Drive file list. Before upload, the backup is encrypted on your device with AES-256, so the file sitting in your Drive is unreadable without the key. The key is a random value generated once per account and stored with our backend provider (Supabase), protected so that only your signed-in account can fetch it; it is never written to your device or to Google Drive. This means Google, or anyone who gets hold of the Drive file alone, cannot read your diary. It is not end-to-end encrypted in the zero-knowledge sense, though: because the key is held by our backend provider rather than derived from a secret only you know, it is not a substitute for a passphrase-protected vault. Backups made by older versions of the app were not encrypted; they are re-uploaded in encrypted form the next time a backup runs.

Crash and diagnostic data. We use Sentry to catch crashes and errors. This can include your device type, OS version, IP address, and — for a sample of sessions (10% of normal sessions, 100% of sessions that hit an error) — a masked screen recording (text and images are masked by default).

Device permissions.

  • Photo library: used to pick a profile photo or embed images in a diary page. Selected photos stay on your device (and are included in backup uploads only if you enable backup).
  • Biometrics / PIN: if you set an app lock, your PIN and whether biometric unlock is enabled are stored locally only.
  • Notifications: used only for the optional journaling reminders you configure, scheduled entirely on-device.

How We Use Information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To sync your profile across devices you sign into.
  • To back up your diary to your own Google Drive, only when you ask us to.
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs.
  • To send the reminder notifications you set up.

We do not use your diary content for analytics, advertising, or any purpose beyond storing and displaying it back to you.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase (authentication, profile storage) — supabase.com/privacy
  • Google (Sign-In, Drive backup storage) — policies.google.com/privacy. Our use of Google user data via Sign-In complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Sentry (crash reporting, session replay) — sentry.io/privacy

Data Retention and Deletion

Diary content lives on your device until you delete it there, or delete a backup copy from your Google Drive's app data directly. Account and profile data (email, name, bio, etc.) is retained until you delete your account.

To delete your account and associated data:

  • In the app: open Profile and tap Delete Account. This immediately removes your account credential (email/password or Google sign-in link) and your profile (name, bio, age, photo) from Supabase.
  • If you can't open the app: email shiva.zen.dev@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it manually.

Deleting your account does not touch diary entries already stored locally on a device, or a Google Drive backup already sitting in your own Drive. To remove those yourself:

  • Local diary entries: uninstall the app, or clear its data from your device's app settings.
  • Google Drive backup: remove the DearDiary app data from Google Drive, or revoke DearDiary's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children's Privacy

DearDiary is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your Choices

  • Backup is opt-in — leave it off and your diary never leaves your device.
  • You can edit or remove most profile fields at any time from the Profile screen.
  • You can disable notifications from your device's system settings.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, note it in the app.
